Compliance

Electrical work must follow Swedish authorization rules

I hold Swedish AL authorization. Regulated electrical installation work on another party's electrical installation is performed only within the legal framework that applies to registered electrical installation companies and their self-audit programme. Where a requested job requires such a company setup, that requirement is confirmed before work is accepted.

Engineering advice, planning, documentation, system assessment and cross-discipline technical consulting can be scoped separately.

Cross-discipline

Electrical, IT, automation and smart-home systems together

Many modern projects are not purely electrical. Lighting controls, gateways, sensors, network connectivity, remote access and automation often meet at the same installation. The advantage of a cross-discipline approach is that these interfaces are considered before equipment is selected or changes are made.

Typical assignments

From fault finding to technical planning

Typical assignments can start with an unclear fault, an installation that needs technical review, a renovation where electrical and control systems must be coordinated, or a smart-home project that needs a realistic electrical and network plan before equipment is purchased.

The first step is to define the problem, constraints and responsibility boundaries. From there I can document findings, propose a technical path, identify dependencies between electrical equipment and connected systems, and clarify which parts require work through a registered electrical installation company.
