Privacy policy and contact data

When you use the contact form on shtogryn.com, the information you enter — such as your name, email address, selected service and message — is used to receive and respond to your enquiry.

The contact form is transmitted through FormSubmit, an external form-delivery service. Do not send passwords, access tokens, payment-card data, national identification numbers or other sensitive credentials through the form.

The website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand visits, page usage and interactions. Standard analytics and hosting systems may process technical information such as page URL, browser and device information, approximate region, request time and IP-related connection data according to their own processing rules and applicable consent settings.

This website is designed as a static site and does not store contact-form submissions in its own browser-side database. Hosting and content delivery infrastructure may process standard connection data required to deliver and protect the site.

External links, including GitHub, Sweden No Sugar and Wise Quotes World, lead to separate services or websites with their own privacy practices.

For questions about this page or your contact data, email alex.shtogryn@gmail.com.
