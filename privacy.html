Privacy policy and contact data

When you use the contact form on shtogryn.com, the information you enter — such as your name, email address, selected service and message — is used to receive and respond to your enquiry.

The contact form is processed through the first-party /api/contact endpoint on Cloudflare. Cloudflare Turnstile is used to verify that the request is legitimate, and the validated message is delivered through the Brevo transactional email API. Contact details are used for the enquiry only and are not automatically added to a marketing list. Do not send passwords, access tokens, payment-card data, national identification numbers or other sensitive credentials through the form.

The website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand visits, page usage and interactions. Standard analytics and hosting systems may process technical information such as page URL, browser and device information, approximate region, request time and IP-related connection data according to their own processing rules and applicable consent settings.

This website does not store contact-form submissions in a browser-side database. During submission, Cloudflare may process standard request and anti-abuse data such as IP-related connection information, and Brevo processes the name, reply email, selected service and message only to deliver the transactional email. The delivered message is then available in the recipient email account.

Analytics is optional. Until you accept analytics cookies, the Google Analytics script is not loaded. If you reject analytics, the site stores only your consent preference locally in your browser so the choice can be respected on later visits. Advertising-related consent remains disabled.

You can reopen Cookie settings from the footer at any time and change the analytics choice. The consent preference is stored in browser localStorage and is not used as a marketing identifier.

The site does not use the analytics consent choice to create an advertising profile, and advertising consent remains denied in the current configuration. If the analytics setup changes materially in the future, this page should be updated before the new processing is enabled.

External links, including GitHub, Sweden No Sugar and Wise Quotes World, lead to separate services or websites with their own privacy practices.

For questions about this page or your contact data, email alex.shtogryn@gmail.com.
