How contact data is handled

When you use the contact form on shtogryn.com, the information you enter — such as your name, email address, selected service and message — is used only to receive and respond to your enquiry.

The contact form is transmitted through FormSubmit, an external form-delivery service. Do not send passwords, access tokens, payment-card data, national identification numbers or other sensitive credentials through the form.

This website is currently designed as a static site and does not store contact-form submissions in its own browser-side database. Technical hosting and delivery may process standard connection data such as IP address, request time and browser information as part of normal web-service operation.

External links, including GitHub, Sweden No Sugar and Wise Quotes World, lead to separate services or websites with their own privacy practices.

For questions about this page or your contact data, email alex.shtogryn@gmail.com.
